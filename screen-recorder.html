Screen Recorder
You design your recording while you're recording.

The Idea

This started as a personal project. I wanted smooth zoom-in transitions and basic interactions I could control myself, while recording — that's how it started.

The Goal

There are hundreds of screen recording tools out there already. The goal was never to compete with those.

The goal was to make the interaction and experience so easy for users that it gives them a final, finished video recording, minimising the decisions they have to make post-recording. You design your recording while you're recording.

The Challenge

The actual challenge was designing this inside that small extension window itself — without making it complex, but still giving real features.

Most screen recording extensions I've seen have a full cloud version behind them; the extension is just there to start recording, pick the mic, and so on. Not many of them let you actually control everything from inside the extension.

Tools Used
