Problem

How do we make verifying an AI's claim about a legal document fast and safe?

Scope

This quick prototype focused on designing the claim verification experience for an AI assisted contract review tool. Specifically, the moment where a lawyer reviews a document, sees the AI's flagged issues, and needs to confirm whether each one is accurate before acting on it. Within that, the design covers:

  • How flagged claims are organized and displayed, grouped by type: deviations from policy versus gaps where no policy exists
  • How a claim is verified against its real source, with the source clearly identified and traceable
  • A clear separation between reading a claim and formally verifying or rejecting it
  • Keeping the document completely untouched by the AI, so the user stays in full ownership and control of their own work
  • Visual progress tracking, so a lawyer can see at a glance how much review is still outstanding
