How long can the process take?

USCIS processing times change and depend on your case, so we won't promise you a date. If you pay for premium processing, USCIS must act on your petition within 15 business days. If USCIS asks for more evidence, the clock stops, and a new 15 business days starts when USCIS gets your answer. After approval, how long you wait depends on your country.

The 10 criteria USCIS looks at

You need one major international award, like a Nobel Prize, or evidence for at least 3 of these 10 criteria.

Not sure which of these you already meet? Frank can check your resume against all ten.

Meeting 3 criteria is step one

In step two, USCIS looks at all your evidence together and decides whether you're truly among the best in your field, and have been for a while. If one of these criteria doesn't fit your kind of work, you can show other evidence of equal weight and explain why. This doesn't apply to the one major award.
