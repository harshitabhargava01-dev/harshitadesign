Questions about using Oval

1. What does Oval check?

Oval scans every slide in your Google Slides™ presentation for colors and fonts that don't match your brand guidelines, and flags low contrast between text and backgrounds.

2. How do I set my brand colors and fonts?

Click the settings icon (⚙️) in the Oval sidebar, then add your brand hex codes under Brand Colors and your font names under Brand Fonts.

3. What does "Fix" do?

Fix automatically replaces the detected off-brand color or font with the closest brand-approved alternative.

4. Why is a fix marked "Manual"?

Table cell colors and fonts cannot be changed automatically — you'll need to update those by hand in Google Slides™.
